A comprehensive framework for the upcoming Nexus ecosystem expansion. This document outlines core architectural decisions and strategic milestones.

Executive Summary

The Nexus expansion aims to redefine how cross-functional teams collaborate within the enterprise space. By leveraging Asymmetric Workflow Design, we reduce cognitive load while maximizing output velocity.

Our primary objective is to move away from the "grid-and-border" constraints that have historically limited digital workspace flexibility.
