# App Review notes — NEXUS 0.1.6 (paste into App Store Connect › App Review Information › Notes)

NEXUS is the internal workspace app for employees of Z Networks (PATS Group): attendance, tasks,
projects, chat and requests. Accounts belong to a company workspace; the demo account below is in a
separate demo workspace with no real company data.

Demo account: apple.review@znetworks.id — password: (same as the previous submission)
The demo account is "location-free", so check-in works from anywhere, including Apple's review site.

HOW TO TRY ATTENDANCE
1. Home › Attendance (or the Attendance bar at the top of Home).
2. Tap "Check in". The app asks for Location "Always" with Precise Location, then a selfie with a short
   head-turn liveness check, then sends the check-in.
3. To check out, write a daily reflection of at least 200 characters, then tap "Check out".

WHY LOCATION "ALWAYS"
Attendance is the core function of the app and the employer's attendance policy requires knowing where
work happens during working hours. Location is used ONLY between the employee's own check-in and
check-out:
- about once an hour while at the office, to show a day spent at the office;
- while the employee is outside the office area, their route, so a site visit or meeting shows where it
  happened; after 2 h 30 min outside without a filed permit they are checked out automatically;
- nothing is recorded before check-in or after check-out, and tracking stops the moment they check out.
The employee sees a Live Activity for the whole workday, including an "outside the office" countdown, and
can see their own route in the app. Location is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.
Background mode "location" is used for exactly this. Without Always, the app explains why and links to
Settings; everything else in the app keeps working.

CAMERA: the check-in and check-out selfie (with an on-device liveness check), and photos attached to
requests, tasks and chat. PHOTOS: attaching images the user picks. NOTIFICATIONS: approvals, reminders and
announcements.

Accounts can be created in the app with a workspace code and deleted in Tuning Room › Delete account.
